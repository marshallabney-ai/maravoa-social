## Caption

```
Maravoa is salon software for the person behind the chair: booth renters, suite and studio owners, and small teams.

Clients book from your own link in any browser. No app download, no account.
Every client has a color card: formula, developer, processing time, technique notes and photos, per visit.
Checkout takes cash, card or Venmo and offers the next visit.
Reports show what you made, beside what you spent, with a tax ready export.

And your book is yours. Clients, color cards and appointment history stay attached to your account, not the salon's. Change salons and all of it moves with you.

Plans start at $19/month, with 14 days free. Click around the live demo first, no login needed. Link in bio.
```
