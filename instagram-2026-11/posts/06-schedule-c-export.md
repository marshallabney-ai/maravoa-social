## Caption

```
Tax season should not start with a shoebox of receipts.

In Maravoa, every service, product and tip lands in your numbers as it happens. Snap a photo of a receipt and the expense logs itself. Each expense category shows the Schedule C line it maps to: rent, supplies, education and the rest.

Come April, export a tax ready CSV and hand it to your accountant. They still do the filing; you just stop rebuilding the year from your bank app.

And none of it lives on the salon's system. It is your business, in your account.

See the reports on the live demo, no login needed. Link in bio.
```
