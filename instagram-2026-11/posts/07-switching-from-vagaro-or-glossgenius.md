## Caption

```
Shopping for salon software? Here is a fair look at three options.

Vagaro is the better pick when you want breadth: a consumer marketplace listing, classes, memberships, multi-location tools and a big add-on catalog. Its base plan is $30.00 a month for one calendar, $23.99 for the first six months, and direct client texting is priced separately.

GlossGenius is the better pick when you want a polished, packaged brand: a designed booking site, a marketing suite, its own card readers and a flat 2.6% processing rate. Standard is $28 a month, or $24 billed annually.

Maravoa is the better pick when you want a focused salon workflow and a book that belongs to you: $19 a month for Stylist ($21 with a texting number) or $29 for Solo with texting included, card checkout to your own Stripe account, color cards for every client, and a client book that moves with you.

Switching? Maravoa imports your client list from a CSV file. You map the columns and review every row before anything is saved.

Competitor prices as checked on their own pricing pages on Sep 1, 2026. Full side by side comparisons are on maravoa.com. Link in bio.
```

## Claim check

Every competitor fact is one the live compare pages state (www.maravoa.com/compare/vagaro and /compare/glossgenius, both "Last verified: Sep 1, 2026", read Nov 8, 2026):

- Vagaro: "$30.00/month ... currently $23.99/month as an exclusive rate for the first six months"; better when breadth matters (marketplace listing, classes, memberships, multi-location tools, add-on ecosystem); "prices direct customer SMS and text marketing separately".
- GlossGenius: "Standard is $28/month, or $24/month billed annually"; "flat 2.6% processing rate and sells its own card readers"; better for "a polished client-facing brand, a packaged booking site, and marketing".
- Maravoa: $19, $21 with texting number, Solo $29 with texting included; checkout to the professional's own Stripe account; color card timeline; portable book (same pages and homepage).
- Client import: feature registry `clients-import-upload`, `clients-import-map`, `clients-import-preview`; the demo screen's own wording. Only the client list is described as imported. Nothing claims appointments come across.
